Graph 1 — Damage distribution

First graph compares the expected number of asset units in each damage state under two different realizations.

The important observations are:
- Realization 0: More buildings remain undamaged.
- Realization 1: Fewer buildings remain undamaged, with more expected in the moderate, extreme, and complete damage states.
- The two realizations represent alternative modeled outcomes, not separate earthquake events to add together.


Graph 2 — Damage-state percentages

Second graph shows exactly the same results, but expressed as percentages rather than absolute quantities.
This makes comparisons much easier because it shows the relative proportion of buildings in each damage state.

For example, Realization 1 has a noticeably larger purple section (complete damage) than Realization 0.
One key distinction: These graphs compare two hazard/model realizations. They are not yet your required experiment comparing original versus modified fragility curves.

Notice that Realization 1 estimates approximately 191,209 more completely damaged units than Realization 0.
